Lembrent-60 and Lembrent-64 Numbering Schemes

This page demonstrates Lembrent-60 (base-60) and Lembrent-64 (base-64) numbering schemes. These systems assign each digit a unique glyph and a name, constructed from consonant bits ("l", "m", "b", "r", "n", "t") and vowels as per the base60 project. The schemes use glyphs with arms encoded as a 6-bit bitmap, and names constructed consistently from bit values (see lembrent/lembrent for details). For every integer, the notation is available in decimal, Lembrent-60 (using an s prefix), and Lembrent-64 (using a k prefix).
